Cyan is visibly blue in this repeat; the missing-cyan concern from the earlier print should not be carried forward as an explanation for this photograph.

The reverse order shows a possible yellow/olive shift, and some magenta/yellow samples show a possible orange shift. The photograph does not demonstrate convincing green/red blends or establish measured colour accuracy. Phone exposure, lighting and viewing direction prevent quantitative calibration from this image alone.

This was an exploratory test of transmission through solid walls of 0.4 or 0.8 mm. It does test one legitimate iDither-style recipe: a pure outer filament over a different pure inner filament. The previously inspected single-model iDither Grinch export used cyan outside and yellow inside for its predicted green. The test therefore should not be dismissed as unrelated to iDither. However, it does not reproduce the full measured construction or the independently ordered outer/inner sequences available in other recipes. Its wall dimensions and generic process were not matched to an ELEGOO-specific calibrated recipe. A good-looking predicted preview is not evidence that these particular reels will produce that shade.

Do not repeat the same comparison unchanged, send the user back to the already-tested generic alternating-layer approach, or mark this pack calibrated. The next experiment should target a documented iDither-style outer/inner construction with matched wall widths, layer schedule, backing and actual ELEGOO product identity. Verify the resulting outer/inner toolpaths before requesting a small physical comparison. Include pure-colour controls and assess ordinary and transmitted light separately. The current negative result does not by itself disprove iDither's broader method.

The [iDither documentation](https://idither.com/wiki.php) describes ordered layer recipes and outer/inner wall combinations. Neither establishes that this solid-wall test should look green in normal room lighting. The observed result does not isolate filament, geometry or slicing as the sole cause.
