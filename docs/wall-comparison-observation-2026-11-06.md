# ELEGOO wall-comparison observation — 6 November 2026

The repeat-print photograph shows the ten-sample **solid outer/inner wall comparison**, including the pure-colour controls. It does not show the separate native alternating-layer comparison. Cyan is visibly blue in this repeat; the missing-cyan concern from the earlier print should not be carried forward as an explanation for this photograph.

The outer wall largely determines the visible colour. Cyan-outside/yellow-inside samples remain blue. The reverse order shows a possible yellow/olive shift, and some magenta/yellow samples show a possible orange shift. The photograph does not demonstrate convincing green/red blends or establish measured colour accuracy. Phone exposure, lighting and viewing direction prevent quantitative calibration from this image alone.

This was an exploratory test of transmission through solid walls of 0.4 or 0.8 mm. Its purpose should have been explained more clearly before printing. It is not a reproduction of iDither's calibrated, ordered layer sequences across outer and inner walls. A good-looking predicted preview is not evidence that these particular reels will produce that shade.

Do not repeat the same comparison unchanged or mark this pack calibrated. A useful next experiment must first verify the sliced colour sequence, use thin controlled layers, include pure-colour and reciprocal cyan/yellow controls, and compare samples in both ordinary and transmitted light. Review any already-printed native-layer comparison before asking for another print.

The manufacturer's [PLA CMYK description](https://www.elegoo.com/en-gb/products/pla-cmyk) presents illuminated lithophanes as an intended application. The [iDither documentation](https://idither.com/wiki.php) describes ordered layer recipes and outer/inner wall combinations. Neither establishes that this solid-wall test should look green in normal room lighting. The observed result does not isolate filament, geometry or slicing as the sole cause.

No printer was started and no optical calibration was added as part of the 2.6.46 modifier-import fix.
