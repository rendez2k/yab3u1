# Guided steps (2.6.79)

The Workbench used to show six equal tabs and put its main button in the header. It was not clear what to do next. This release arranges the same tools as four steps.

| Step | What it asks | The bar's button |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Model | Which file, which printer | Next: Colours |
| 2 Colours | How should the colours print? | Apply palette, then Next: Check |
| 3 Check | Is this what you want written? | Download (or a list of downloads) |
| 4 Download | — | Jumps to step 3's button |

## Step 2

One sentence states the situation, for example "Your model uses 8 colours. Your printer holds 4 at once. Choose how to print the other 4." Three cards follow:

- **Blend the extra colours** — Full Spectrum blends.
- **Merge similar colours** — solid colours.
- **Swap reels mid-print** — the U1 reel-change plan (Snapmaker output only).

Each method keeps its own choices, so switching between them to compare loses nothing.

## What moved

- Paint, Split and Plates: under **Edit** in the rail.
- Print settings: folded under **Print options · advanced** (blends) or **Print settings · advanced** (solid colours) in step 3.
- "Keep all project filaments for further slicer setup": step 2, under the cards.

## Checked

Walked through in a headless browser at desktop and phone widths, for both colour methods, including downloads from the bar. The header's Export button still works as before.
