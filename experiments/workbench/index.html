How the Workbench works

Your model stays on screen while you colour, split, turn and arrange it. Everything runs in this browser; nothing is uploaded.

The tools

  • Project: open a model and choose the slicer and printer it is going to.
  • Colours: assign filaments, merge colours, or explore Full Spectrum blends.
  • Paint: recolour whole objects or one of their colours.
  • Split: choose a method, preview the pieces, then add connectors.
  • Plates: switch plates, add or remove copies, arrange, fill, and prepare sequential groups.
  • Settings: review print settings and send filament assignments to a U1.
  • Export (top right): this plate, every plate in one 3MF, or a ZIP of separate print jobs.

Working with objects

  • Click an object to select it. Select multiple, Shift-click or Ctrl/Cmd-click picks several.
  • Move: drag a selected object across the plate. Positions stay fixed until you choose Auto arrange; new copies pack around them.
  • Turn & resize: lay an object flat on its largest face, tip or spin it by any angle, or resize it from 10% to 1000%. All copies change together and keep their painting.
  • Edits are previews until you choose Apply. Cancel leaves the model unchanged.
Splitting in detail
  • Automatic finds larger assembly parts or painted regions and separates those that close safely.
  • By painted colour follows a colour boundary; choose one connected region. Plane cut slices straight through. By shape and Draw boundary let you pick the region yourself.
  • Connectors: an attached pin, a separate dowel with two sockets, or sockets for your own pin. Check join shows the mating face and material depth. Printed fit still needs a small test.
  • Repair mesh fixes duplicate faces and pinched joins before splitting, moving surfaces by at most 0.001 mm. Holes and conflicting paint may need another tool.

Keyboard

  • Ctrl/Cmd+O open a model
  • Ctrl/Cmd+Z undo
  • Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y redo plate and model edits
  • Ctrl/Cmd+A select every object
  • Delete remove selected copies
  • Esc clear the selection or cancel an edit

Good to know

  • There is one workspace. Simple view in the App menu hides the tool rail and shows only the destination and export settings, for a straight conversion.
  • Tips at the foot of the tool rail shows or hides the explanations in each panel. Status and warning messages always stay visible.
  • Solid colours and Full Spectrum blends are two colour methods for the same model, chosen in Colours. Each keeps its own colour choices; model edits and hand-placed positions carry across.
  • Previews are approximations. Your slicer validates toolpaths, supports and clearance.

Your last model

Reopening restores the model with its applied edits, destination, plate and quantities. Colour assignments and Full Spectrum choices come back too. Unapplied previews are not kept, and nothing is uploaded. Export your work to keep it elsewhere.
