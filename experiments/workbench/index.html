A clearer way to prepare your print

Your model stays visible while you select, colour, split and arrange its parts.

Repair mesh: in Split & connect, fix duplicate faces and suitable pinched joins before splitting. Retained triangles keep their painting; local adjustments are limited to 0.001 mm. The edited project is checked before applying. Undo restores the original. Holes, conflicting paint and ambiguous joins may need a different repair tool.

Model tools: select copies, preview a colour change or split the selected objects, then Apply. Split can detect shape regions for selection, recolouring and validated separation. Combine regions with the selection buttons, or Shift/Ctrl-click the model. Draw boundary places editable points on the original mesh. Close the loop, move or remove points, and preview the closed pieces assembled or apart before applying. Curved joins may still need a plane cut. Split also follows a painted colour boundary, cuts on a plane, or separates disconnected pieces. Choose one connected region of a shared colour; unsupported boundaries need editing in your slicer. Keep both parts or remove the region. Add an attached pin, a separate printable dowel with two sockets, or sockets for your own pin. Check join shows the mating face and checks material depth; move the connector there or with precise offsets. Attached pins can face upwards and separate dowels stand upright. Printed fit still needs a small test. New parts can be coloured or arranged immediately. Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes plate changes, then model edits.

  1. Project: open a model and choose its destination.
  2. Colours: assign filaments, merge colours or explore Full Spectrum.
  3. Plates: switch plates, delete or restore a plate, duplicate or remove objects. Arrange, fill and sequential groups are here too.
  4. Settings: review print settings and send U1 filament assignments.
  5. Export (top right): download this plate, every plate in one 3MF, or a ZIP of separate print jobs.

Tips at the foot of the tool rail shows or hides the explanations in each panel. Status and warning messages always stay visible.

Keyboard: Ctrl/Cmd+O opens a model · Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes · Ctrl/Cmd+A selects every object · Shift-click adds to the selection · Delete removes selected copies · Esc clears the selection.

Convert and Full Spectrum keep separate colour experiments in this tab. Switching back keeps that mode's experiment. Applying a model edit creates a new source revision; the other mode loads that revision on your next visit.

Reopening restores the model with its applied edits, destination, plate and quantities. Colour assignments and Full Spectrum choices come back too. Unapplied previews are not kept, and nothing is uploaded. Download your work before leaving. Previews are approximations; your slicer validates toolpaths and clearance.
