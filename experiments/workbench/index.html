A clearer way to prepare your print

This is a separate YAB3D test version. Your model stays visible while the task panel changes.

Model tools: select copies, preview a colour change or split the selected objects, then Apply. Split follows a painted colour boundary, cuts on a plane, or separates disconnected pieces. Choose one connected region of a shared colour; unsupported boundaries need editing in your slicer. Keep both parts or remove the region. Add a locating pin and socket to join the parts again; pins are oriented upwards by default. New parts can be coloured or arranged immediately. Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes plate changes, then model edits.

  1. Project: open a model and choose its destination.
  2. Colours: assign filaments, merge colours or explore Full Spectrum.
  3. Layout: select, duplicate, remove and fill; prepare separate colour plates or sequential groups.
  4. Output: review settings, export and send U1 filament assignments.

Convert and Full Spectrum keep separate colour experiments in this tab. Switching back keeps that mode's experiment. Applying a model edit creates a new source revision; the other mode loads that revision on your next visit.

Projects are not saved after closing or reloading this tab. Download your work before leaving. Previews are approximations; your slicer validates toolpaths and clearance.
