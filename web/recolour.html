Print with manual reel changes · U1 experimental

Keep more than four original colours by changing reels between layers. Start with an all-PLA model and a U1 with 0.4 mm nozzles. The slice must fit four colours per layer.

  1. Load your original 3MF above and download a slice-only project. This keeps its original colours, regardless of recolouring choices above.
  2. Open it as a project in OrcaSlicer with U1 profiles, check its settings, slice and export G-code. Do not print that virtual-tool slice.
  3. Choose the sliced file below. Prepare a U1 file with pauses and download its reel-change sheet.

The export remaps tools, temperatures and purge commands to four heads and inserts M600 pauses. At each pause, load and purge the listed reels using the printer controls before resuming. Test a short print with at least two pauses first; this YAB3D exporter has not yet been verified on a physical printer.

Colour prediction is an uncalibrated estimate from swatch values. Reel-change exports need a real U1 slice. Nothing here slices for you or sends files to your printer.

Blend predictions use FilamentMixer by Justin Hayes, via Strata. Credits and third-party notices.
