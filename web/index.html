3MF converter

yab3u1 — Yet Another Bloody 3MF-to-U1 converter. Now moving painted 3MFs between Snapmaker Orca, Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer and PrusaSlicer — in any direction. Every filament definition, the paint and the geometry travel unchanged: no substitutions, no blends, no four-slot limit. Runs entirely in your browser — your model is never uploaded.

Drop a .3mf here or click to choose a file — PrusaSlicer, Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer or Snapmaker Orca projects

Write a project

Filament assignment

Every colour keeps its own filament. Change a row to send that colour to another filament, or exchange two of them at once. This assignment is the only thing that changes a colour.

Plate layout

How many copies to write and the box they are arranged in. These are layout dimensions for this file — not a printer bed; pick your printer in the slicer after importing.

Print settings

Preview optional

An approximate look at the output, drawn from the simplified surface in the colours this export would write, with sub-divided facets shown in their dominant colour. It is a model preview, not sliced toolpaths, and it never leaves your machine.

Full Spectrum recolouring separate step

Recolour a painted model onto your own four reels, compare predicted filament blends and write a Snapmaker Orca, Bambu Studio or PrusaSlicer project. Optional — the converter above works on its own.
