YAB3D 3MF converter

YAB3D — Yet Another Bloody 3D Tool. Convert · recolour · arrange. Now moving painted 3MFs between Snapmaker Orca, Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer and PrusaSlicer — in any direction. Selected filament definitions, the paint and the geometry travel unchanged: no substitutions, no blends, no four-slot limit. Runs entirely in your browser — your model is never uploaded.

From MakerWorld? Get the Chrome extension

Download Chrome extension 1.5.3.6 (ZIP) — review build. Open the original 3MF in Analyse & convert or Full Spectrum.

  1. Extract the ZIP to a folder you will keep.
  2. Open chrome://extensions, switch on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked and select the extracted folder.
  3. Refresh MakerWorld, choose a print profile, then click Open in YAB3D. Allow its new tab and complete MakerWorld verification if prompted.

Already using our updated extension? Reload it in Chrome and refresh MakerWorld. This package opens the YAB3D review site. Original model data transfers between tabs; it is not uploaded.

    Batch details and limits

    Files run one at a time. Failed files or plates are listed in the report; successful ones still download. Completed outputs are limited to 256 MB per ZIP. Larger jobs can be split into smaller batches.

    Thumbnails are included. Native filament blends require individual review. U1 plates must fit its bed with planning clearance; for other formats, choose your printer and check bed fit in the slicer. Stopping keeps completed outputs and lists everything left unprocessed.
