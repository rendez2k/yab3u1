YAB3D 3MF converter

YAB3D — Yet Another Bloody 3D Tool. Convert · recolour · arrange. Now moving painted 3MFs between Snapmaker Orca, Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer and PrusaSlicer — in any direction. Every filament definition, the paint and the geometry travel unchanged: no substitutions, no blends, no four-slot limit. Runs entirely in your browser — your model is never uploaded.
